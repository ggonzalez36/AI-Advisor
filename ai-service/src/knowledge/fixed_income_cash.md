# Fixed Income and Cash Management (synthetic sample)

> Synthetic document for demonstration purposes. Not investment advice.

## Role of fixed income
Investment grade bonds reduce portfolio volatility and provide income. For conservative
investors and short horizons, fixed income is the primary capital preservation tool.

## Cash drag
Holding more than 20% of a portfolio in idle cash creates cash drag that erodes real
returns over long horizons. Excess cash should be deployed gradually into the target
allocation, typically via low-cost index ETFs.

## Liquidity reserve
Keep a cash cushion of roughly 10% of AUM for near-term needs and rebalancing, adjusted
to the client's risk band.
