# Equity Concentration Risk (synthetic sample)

> Synthetic document for demonstration purposes. Not investment advice.

## Single-stock and sector concentration
Portfolios with more than 20% in one stock or more than 40% in one sector (for example
semiconductors) carry idiosyncratic risk that diversification would otherwise remove.
Historical drawdowns for concentrated technology holdings have exceeded 50%.

## Mitigation
Reduce concentration gradually by directing new cash to broad market index funds such
as VOO or VTI. Consider tax impact before selling appreciated positions; redirecting
contributions is often preferable to immediate liquidation.

## Monitoring
Track the largest position weight and the Herfindahl concentration index monthly.
A largest position above 20% of AUM requires a documented rationale.
