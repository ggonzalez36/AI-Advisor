# Suitability and Human Oversight Rules (synthetic sample)

> Synthetic document for demonstration purposes. Not investment advice.

## Suitability
Recommendations must match the client's declared risk tolerance and investment horizon.
Crypto assets are unsuitable for CONSERVATIVE and BALANCED clients under any circumstance.
A recommendation that contradicts the declared tolerance must be rejected, not executed.

## Mandatory human review
A licensed advisor must approve the proposal before execution when any of these apply:
- The portfolio risk score is 0.70 or higher.
- A single trade exceeds 10% of AUM.
- The recommendation was produced by a degraded or fallback model path.
- A proposed trade would leave any single security above the concentration limit.

## Explainability and audit
Every AI-generated recommendation must cite the internal documents it relied on.
Recommendations citing sources that were not retrieved are treated as hallucinations
and rejected automatically. All decisions, approvals and rejections are logged.
