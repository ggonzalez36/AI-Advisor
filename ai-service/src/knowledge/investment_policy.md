# Investment Policy Statement (synthetic sample)

> Synthetic document for demonstration purposes. Not investment advice.

## Strategic allocation bands by risk tolerance
Target allocations are expressed as a share of total assets under management (AUM).
- CONSERVATIVE: 20% equity, 60% fixed income, 20% cash equivalents. No crypto exposure.
- BALANCED: 50% equity, 35% fixed income, 15% cash equivalents. No crypto exposure.
- GROWTH: 75% equity, 15% fixed income, 10% cash equivalents. Crypto up to 0% by default.
- AGGRESSIVE: 85% equity, 5% fixed income, 5% cash equivalents, crypto capped at 5%.

## Concentration limits
No single security may exceed 20% of AUM after a proposed trade. Broad index ETFs
(VOO, VTI, VEA, BND, AGG) are exempt from this limit and are the preferred instruments
for rebalancing because they diversify idiosyncratic risk at low cost.

## Rebalancing rules
Rebalance when an asset class drifts more than 5 percentage points from its target.
Trades smaller than 1,000 USD are skipped to avoid unnecessary transaction costs.
Purchases must be funded by available cash; the advisor never proposes leverage.

## Long horizon glide path
Investors with horizons above 15 years may hold up to 10 percentage points more equity
than their band, funded from fixed income. Horizons under 3 years should move 10
percentage points from equity to cash equivalents.
